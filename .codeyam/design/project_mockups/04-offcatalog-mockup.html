How you get in

Open registration
First come, first served until the field fills. Popular NYRR races can sell out within hours.
Lottery
Apply during the window, then wait for the drawing. Applying early doesn’t change your odds, but missing the deadline ends them.NYC Half 2027: closes Tue, Nov 17, 11:59 pm ET
Time qualifier
A qualifying time run inside a set window, tied to a specific race edition.
Charity
Guaranteed entry for a fundraising commitment with an official partner.
Membership or prior races
Club membership or races completed the year before, like NYRR’s 9+1.
