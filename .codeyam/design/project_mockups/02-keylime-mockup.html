Five ways in

Lottery

Apply inside the window, then wait for the drawing. Applying early does not improve your odds. Missing the deadline ends your chances.

Open

First come, first served. Popular races fill in hours.

Time qualifier

A fast enough time, in the window for that edition.

Charity

Guaranteed entry for a fundraising pledge.

Membership

Club membership or prior-year races, like NYRR’s 9+1 program.
