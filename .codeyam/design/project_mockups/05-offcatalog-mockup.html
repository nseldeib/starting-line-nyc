How you get in

Open
First come, first served until it fills.
Lottery
Apply in the window. Early doesn’t mean better odds.
Time qualifier
A fast enough time, in the window for that edition.
Charity
Guaranteed entry for a fundraising pledge.
Membership
Club membership or prior-year races, like NYRR’s 9+1.
